You can plan and visualise a room without paying. These are the tools we reviewed that are free or have a free plan, along with the catch in each.

How we chose

We included tools with a free plan or a free licence and noted the limits the vendor states (project caps, watermarks, non-commercial use).

1. Sweet Home 3D — Our pick

Free and open source, for drawing a plan, placing furniture and viewing it in 3D. Runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android and in a browser. The simplest no-cost start.
