Best Interior Design Software in 2026

Last updated 2026-10-10 · By the DesignTested team

Choosing interior design software is mostly a question of what you need to hand a client: a floor plan, a photorealistic render, a proposal, or all three. This guide groups the main tools by that job and lists the prices published on each vendor’s own site.

Based on prices published on each vendor’s own site (12 October 2026). We have not yet tested these tools hands-on; the full reviews state what is verified.

Foyr Neo

Foyr Neo is aimed at interior designers who want to go from a floor plan to a rendered scene quickly. Its published plans are Basic ($39/month), Standard ($89/month) and Premium ($159/month), with a 14-day free trial on all plans. Plans differ mainly in monthly render credits and in seats; the pricing page shows different credit numbers in different places, so check the current allowance for your plan before you subscribe.

RoomSketcher

RoomSketcher covers 2D and 3D floor plans, 3D photos and 360 views. The Free plan allows one user and a 10-project archive, with watermarked snapshots. Pro costs $24/month, or $12/month billed as $144 per year, and adds the full furniture library, custom branding and print to scale. Team ($70/month, or $35/month billed as $420 per year) adds five users and collaboration.

SketchUp

SketchUp is the most flexible general 3D modeller on this list, and also the steepest to learn. Annual-billed plans: Go $10.75/month (iPad and web), Pro $33.25/month (desktop modeller, LayOut documentation, extensions), Studio $71.58/month (Windows, adds photorealistic visuals and animation). Education plans start at $55 per user per year.

Sweet Home 3D

Sweet Home 3D is free, open-source software for drawing a plan, placing furniture and viewing a 3D result. It runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android, and online in a WebGL-capable browser. It is a good way to sketch layouts at no cost; it will not produce the polished renders that paid tools do.

Planner 5D

Planner 5D has a free plan with access to half of its furniture catalogue, plus Premium and Professional tiers. The FAQ lists Premium at $19.99/month or $59.99 billed annually, and Professional at $49.99/month or $399.99 billed annually; the pricing cards show different headline figures, so confirm at checkout. Professional includes unlimited 4K renders and CAD export.

Cedreo

Cedreo offers Free, Personal, Pro and Enterprise plans. The Free plan is limited to one project; Personal projects include 20 rendering credits each; Pro provides monthly credits and annual billing. The pricing page does not show amounts in the text we could read, so see the Cedreo pricing page for current figures.

How to choose

  1. Client-facing renders are your main deliverable: start with Foyr Neo’s free trial and compare against Planner 5D Professional.
  2. You mostly need accurate plans and measurements: RoomSketcher Pro is the cheapest paid option here.
  3. You build custom furniture or detailed models: SketchUp Pro.
  4. You are just starting out or want to experiment: Sweet Home 3D or the free tiers of RoomSketcher and Planner 5D cost nothing.
