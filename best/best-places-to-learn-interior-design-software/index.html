Best Places to Learn Interior Design Software in 2026

Published 2026-09-30 · Facts checked 2026-10-15 · By the DesignTested team

Most design software makers publish their own training: video tutorials, written manuals and help centres. They are the best place to learn a tool’s real workflow, because they are written for the current version. This roundup lists official resources only, not third-party courses.

How we chose

We picked six learning resources, each hosted on the software maker’s own website and read on 2026-10-15. Everything below is based on what those pages publish. We have not hands-on tested any of the courses or tutorials, and we do not rate them. Cost is stated only where the page says it; otherwise the table says “Not published”. Note that SketchUp’s Trimble Learn pages did not display any content when we fetched them, so we did not include them and list the SketchUp Help Center instead.

1. D5 Tutorials

Our pick. On published information it is the only resource here that is described as free and that can be filtered by industry, including interior design, as well as by skill level, language and the software you work in (for example SketchUp, Blender, Revit or Archicad). That fits designers who already model elsewhere and want to learn real-time rendering. This is a judgement from the page, not from testing the tutorials.

D5 describes its tutorials page as free video tutorials, tips and workflow guides from the D5 Creative Team, certified instructors and the community. Filters include “For beginners”, “For advanced users” and “For Students”, and a language list that includes English, Chinese, French, Spanish and others. Formats include full tutorials and one-minute tutorials.

2. Chief Architect Training Videos

Chief Architect’s video page groups its library by topic: quick start, user interface, walls, doors and windows, cabinets, kitchens and baths, rooms and floors, dimensions, materials, rendering with cameras and lighting, layouts and construction documents, and import and export. It also lists NKBA CEU approved videos and recorded webinars.

The company’s support page lists the training videos among its self-help resources, alongside a reference manual, a tutorial guide, a knowledge base and a user forum. It also says its On-Demand Residential Introductory Class was “currently available for free ($495 value)”. The software itself is paid; its price is not on these pages.

Chief Architect Training Videos

3. Live Home 3D video tutorials

Live Home 3D’s tutorial page for the Mac version lists short videos on the user interface, importing a home plan, creating a floor plan, working with objects, applying materials, adding roofs, terrain tools and more. For rendering it covers AI Render, Cycles and Radeon ProRender, and a “Creating a House From Start to Finish” video is listed.

Separate tutorial pages exist for iOS and Android, and the company publishes a Quick Start Guide for iPhone and iPad with sections such as drawing walls, adding doors and windows, applying materials and using the RoomScanner. The app has a free version with limits, and Standard and Pro features are sold as in-app purchases, according to the company’s support page.

Live Home 3D Mac tutorials

4. SketchUp Help Center

SketchUp describes its Help Center as the official place to search for tutorials, read release notes, find technical support and get modelling questions answered. The front page has a “Start your journey” block for people new to the software, with learning resources to get up to speed.

Categories cover SketchUp, LayOut (documentation and presentation), SketchUp for Web, SketchUp for iPad, SketchUp for Education, and accounts and administration. Pricing for training is not published on the page.

SketchUp Help Center

5. Blender 5.2 LTS Manual

The official Blender manual is a written reference. Its Getting Started section covers installing Blender, configuring it, and the user interface: windows, workspaces, areas, keymaps and tools. The manual page shown was for Blender 5.2 LTS.

Blender’s own site calls the software the free and open source 3D creation suite. The tutorials page there points to community tutorials tagged #b3d and to Blender Studio for professional training. It suits designers who model and render in Blender and prefer to read rather than watch.

Blender Manual

6. Blender Studio Training

Blender Studio describes its training as courses, workshops and production lessons with .blend files included. Categories include Geometry Nodes, Lighting, Rendering and Shading, and a mini series on customizing material assets for Blender 5.2 is featured on the page.

The Blender Studio home page lists access to all training, assets and films for €11.50/month. The tutorials are aimed at Blender users in general, not specifically at interior design.
