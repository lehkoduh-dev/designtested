Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

This policy explains what data DesignTested collects when you visit the site and what we do with it. We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we collect

  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we keep your message and address to reply to you.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes IP addresses and request data to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Privacy-friendly analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It does not use cookies or track you across sites, and we do not use it to identify individual visitors.
  • Advertising cookies. If we enable advertising or other cookie-based tools, we will ask for your consent first and list the providers here.

Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link, the vendor or affiliate network may set a cookie to credit us for the referral. This is governed by their privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, and you can complain to your national data protection authority (in Latvia: Datu valsts inspekcija).
