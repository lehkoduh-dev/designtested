AutoCAD Review 2026: Pricing, Features and Alternatives

Last updated 2026-10-13 · By the DesignTested team

Quick take

Best for: Designers, architects and engineers who need precise 2D and 3D DWG drafting and documentation.

Not ideal if: Anyone looking for a quick, visual room planner or a render-first tool with ready-made furniture catalogues.

Our verdict: A drafting standard aimed at professional documentation, priced well above consumer room planners; judged only on Autodesk's published information.

Based on the vendor’s published information; hands-on notes will be added.

What it is

Autodesk describes AutoCAD as software for “faster, smarter 2D and 3D drafting”, powered by Autodesk AI to assist with routine tasks. It works with DWG files and is used to produce precise technical drawings rather than quick visual mock-ups.

Who it’s for

According to Autodesk’s FAQ, AutoCAD is used by students, architects, designers, engineers, project managers, real estate developers and construction professionals to create precise 2D and 3D drawings. For interior designers it is most relevant when construction-grade plans, details and drawing sets are required.

Key features

  • 2D and 3D drafting with access to DWG files on desktop, web and mobile.
  • Specialised toolsets listed by Autodesk: Architecture, Mechanical, MEP, Electrical, Plant 3D, Map 3D and Raster Design.
  • Forma Data Management Essentials, listed as included with standalone AutoCAD subscriptions, for shared DWG collaboration and multi-user markups.
  • Newly listed features include Geometry Cleanup, Checkout (work on only the geometry you need), Connected References and Connected Support Files.
  • Autodesk Assistant, shown as a tech preview, for AI-generated support and CAD standards checking.
  • A 15-day free trial.

Pricing

Published on Autodesk’s AutoCAD page, read 13 October 2026 (USD, excluding freight, handling and taxes; Autodesk calls these suggested retail prices that may change):

Plan Price
Annual subscription $2,095 per year (the page also shows “starting at $175 / month”)
Monthly subscription $260 per month

Autodesk also points to a pay-as-you-go option (Flex) and lists a 30-day return policy on annual and multi-year subscriptions and 15 days for monthly ones. The Architecture, Engineering & Construction Collection is promoted as saving up to 80% over individual annual licences.

Pros

  • Focused on precise 2D and 3D drafting and DWG workflows.
  • Industry-specific toolsets, including Architecture and Electrical.
  • Free trial and a stated money-back policy.
  • Cloud collaboration tools included with the subscription.

Cons

  • Priced as a professional tool; the annual price is a large step up from consumer planners.
  • Not built around furniture catalogues or one-click visualisation.
  • Pricing is subject to change and may differ by region.

Alternatives

  • SketchUp for flexible 3D modelling.
  • Enscape for real-time rendering of existing models.
  • D5 Render for visualisation.
