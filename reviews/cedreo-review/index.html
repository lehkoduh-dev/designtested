Cedreo Review 2026: Pricing, Features and Alternatives

Last updated 2026-10-12 · By the DesignTested team

Quick take

Best for: Home builders, remodelers and designers who need floor plans, plan sets and renderings for client projects, and homeowners with a single project (the Personal plan).

Not ideal if: Teams that need to downgrade plans or switch back to monthly billing later, and anyone looking mainly for interior-styling tools, since the vendor describes Cedreo as software for home builders, contractors and remodelers.

Our verdict: Cedreo publishes four clear tiers, from a one-project Free plan to a per-user Enterprise plan, with rendering allowances that scale by plan. Its rules on no downgrades and expiring monthly renderings are worth reading before subscribing.

Based on the vendor’s published information; hands-on notes will be added.

What it is

Cedreo is an online home design tool for drawing floor plans and producing renderings. It offers a Free plan and Personal, Professional and Enterprise tiers, and the FAQ says nothing needs to be downloaded.

Who it’s for

Home builders, remodelers and designers who need house plans and visuals for clients.

Key features

  • Free plan: one project with basic floor plan drawing tools.
  • Personal and Pro plans include rendering allowances; extra renderings can be bought and do not expire at the end of the period, per the FAQ.
  • Pro and Enterprise monthly rendering credits expire at the end of each period.

Pricing

From Cedreo’s pricing page, read in a rendered browser on 13 October 2026 (USD; tax treatment not stated):

Plan Price Includes
Free $0 1 user, 1 project, essential floor plan drawing
Personal $59 per project, pay once 20 renderings and 3 AI renderings per project
Pro $59/month billed monthly, or $39/month billed annually Unlimited projects, 40 renderings/month, 10 AI renders
Enterprise $69/user/month billed monthly, or $59/user/month billed annually Team tools, 80 renderings and 20 AI renderings per user/month

Per the vendor’s FAQ: Free accounts are kept for 3 years and Personal accounts for 10 years unless you upgrade; plans cannot be downgraded after subscribing; and annual billing cannot be switched back to monthly.

Pros

  • Free plan to try the drawing tools.
  • No credit card needed to sign up.
  • Prices, including a one-off per-project Personal option, are published on the pricing page.

Cons

  • Free plan is limited to one project.
  • No downgrades once subscribed.

Alternatives

  • Foyr Neo for interior rendering.
  • RoomSketcher for floor plans.
  • Planner 5D for home design with renders.

FAQ

What payment methods does Cedreo accept? Credit card (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and PayPal, per the vendor’s FAQ (read 13 October 2026).

What happens to unused renderings? On Pro and Enterprise, unused monthly renderings expire and do not roll over. Additional renderings you buy do not expire.

Can I cancel and get a refund? You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period, but the FAQ says no refund is issued for the unused period.
