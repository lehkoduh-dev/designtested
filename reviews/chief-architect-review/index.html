Chief Architect Review 2026: Professional Residential Design and Drafting

What it is

Chief Architect Premier is professional home design software for residential and light commercial design. According to the vendor, drawing walls and placing smart objects such as doors and windows generates a 3D model and a materials list, and supports construction documents including site plans, framing plans, sections and elevations.

Who it’s for

The vendor lists architects and builders, remodelers, interior designers, kitchen and bath designers, academic users and DIY enthusiasts. Its kitchen, bath and interior pages are aimed at interior design professionals.

Key features

  • Automatic 3D model generated as you draw, with simultaneous 2D and 3D editing
  • Photorealistic and artistic rendering styles, including line drawing and watercolor
  • Smart cabinets and manufacturer-partnered objects (cabinets, appliances, doors, windows, countertops, flooring)
  • 3D library of cabinets, appliances, furnishings and textures
  • Elevations, sections, dimensioning, schedules and walkthroughs

Pricing

From the Premier product page, read 12 October 2026 (USD; tax treatment not stated): $1,995 per year billed annually (shown as $166.25/month), or $229/month billed monthly. A legacy-licence upgrade is offered at $995 for the first year. Includes software upgrades, premium catalog downloads, priority support and Chief As-Built app plan import. A trial download is listed.

Pros

  • Covers drafting, 3D modelling and construction documents in one program
  • Interior and kitchen/bath tooling alongside architectural tools
  • Monthly billing is available

Cons

  • Much higher price than most tools in this category
  • Built for construction documentation, which may be more than a decorating-focused designer needs
  • We have not tested the learning curve

Alternatives

See SketchUp, Live Home 3D and Houzz Pro.
