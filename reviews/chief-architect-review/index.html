Chief Architect Review 2026: Professional Residential Design and Drafting

Published 2026-08-20 · Facts checked 2026-10-12 · By the DesignTested team

Quick take

Best for: Architects, builders, remodelers and kitchen and bath or interior designers who need 3D models, schedules and construction documents from one program, per the vendor's listed audience.

Not ideal if: Decorating-focused designers or anyone on a small budget, since the vendor prices Premier at $1,995 per year or $229 per month and positions it around construction documentation.

Our verdict: Chief Architect Premier is a broad drafting, 3D and documentation program with a published subscription price at the high end of this category. It looks aimed at professionals who will use its construction-document tools.

Based on the vendor’s published information; hands-on notes will be added.

What it is

Chief Architect Premier is professional home design software for residential and light commercial design. According to the vendor, drawing walls and placing smart objects such as doors and windows generates a 3D model and a materials list, and supports construction documents including site plans, framing plans, sections and elevations.

Who it’s for

The vendor lists architects and builders, remodelers, interior designers, kitchen and bath designers, academic users and DIY enthusiasts. Its kitchen, bath and interior pages are aimed at interior design professionals.

Key features

  • Automatic 3D model generated as you draw, with simultaneous 2D and 3D editing
  • Photorealistic and artistic rendering styles, including line drawing and watercolor
  • Smart cabinets and manufacturer-partnered objects (cabinets, appliances, doors, windows, countertops, flooring)
  • 3D library of cabinets, appliances, furnishings and textures
  • Elevations, sections, dimensioning, schedules and walkthroughs

Pricing

From the Premier product page, read 12 October 2026 (USD; tax treatment not stated): $1,995 per year billed annually (shown as $166.25/month), or $229/month billed monthly. A legacy-licence upgrade is offered at $995 for the first year. Includes software upgrades, premium catalog downloads, priority support and Chief As-Built app plan import. A trial download is listed. Prices are in USD, per the vendor’s subscription FAQ; the annual subscription is described as saving 27% over monthly (chiefarchitect.com/software-subscription, read 13 October 2026).

Pros

  • Covers drafting, 3D modelling and construction documents in one program
  • Interior and kitchen/bath tooling alongside architectural tools
  • Monthly billing is available

Cons

  • Much higher price than most tools in this category
  • Built for construction documentation, which may be more than a decorating-focused designer needs
  • We have not tested the learning curve

Alternatives

See SketchUp, Live Home 3D and Houzz Pro.

FAQ

Is there a free trial? Yes. The vendor’s FAQ (read 13 October 2026) says the trial has the same tools and features as the paid version except saving, printing, exporting and virtual tours, and that it does not expire.

Does the money-back guarantee apply to subscriptions? Annual subscriptions qualify for the two-week satisfaction guarantee; monthly subscriptions do not.

Can I install it on more than one computer? It is a single-user licence. The software can be installed on several computers for personal use, but only one can be active at a time. Chief Architect is compatible with Mac and PC.
