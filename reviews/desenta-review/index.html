Desenta Review 2026: White-Label Project Delivery for Interior Designers

Last updated 2026-10-13 · By the DesignTested team

Quick take

Best for: Practising interior designers and studios who want to present and deliver a project to clients through one branded, always-current link.

Not ideal if: Designers who need a tool to draw floor plans or create renders, or full business management with proposals and invoicing; Desenta covers project delivery only.

Our verdict: Desenta is our own product, so treat this as a description rather than an independent assessment. We have not compared it with the other tools here; based on the published pricing page it offers a free plan and plans that differ by active project count.

Disclosure: Desenta is our own product. People connected with this site also work on Desenta, so this page is not an independent review. We list it because it fits this audience, and we label it so you can weigh it accordingly.

What it is

Desenta is a white-label platform where an interior designer builds a project in phases and shares it with the client through a single link. According to the official site, the phases are brief and inspiration, concept design (moodboards), visualisations, floor plans, a finishes schedule and an FF&E schedule with budgets.

Who it’s for

Practising interior designers and studios who deliver projects to clients and want one branded, always-current view instead of separate files and emails. It is a delivery and presentation tool: it does not draw floor plans or render scenes. Those are uploaded from other tools (see SketchUp, Foyr Neo, Enscape).

Key features

  • White-label branding of the client space.
  • Moodboard builder with Pinterest import and one-click background removal, or uploads from any other design tool.
  • Visualisations and floor plans uploaded and organised by space.
  • Finishes and FF&E schedules generated by AI from product links.
  • Ready-made presentation templates, version history, client sharing link and ZIP download.

Pros

  • Free plan to try it on a real project.

  • Built for the delivery step that drawing and rendering tools leave out.

Cons

  • It does not create plans or renders itself.
  • Young product: Early Access at the time of writing.

FAQ

These answers come from the pricing page of desenta.com (read 13 October 2026), written by the vendor, which is us.

What counts as an active project? Projects you are currently working on. You can archive completed projects to free up slots, and archived projects remain accessible to you and your clients.

Can I switch plans later? Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel, and changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

What is included in Early Access? Unlimited projects, unlimited storage and all features until public launch, with founding-member pricing promised to Early Access members.

Alternatives

For business management with proposals and invoicing, see Houzz Pro and DesignFiles.
