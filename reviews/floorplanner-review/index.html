Floorplanner Review 2026: Online 2D Floor Plans and 3D Room Planning

Last updated 2026-10-12 · By the DesignTested team

Based on the vendor’s published information; hands-on notes will be added.

What it is

Floorplanner is an online floor plan and 3D room planning tool. The vendor says it has operated since 2007 and reports over 30 million users and over 50 million projects created.

Who it’s for

The site lists personal use, retail, real estate and interior design as use cases, and offers subscriptions for individuals and teams.

Key features

  • Draw rooms, move walls and add doors and windows in a browser editor, with no installation
  • Real-time 3D view and 3D renders up to 8K resolution, per the homepage
  • A library of over 260,000 3D models, available to all users at no extra cost
  • 2D and 3D image output with default settings meant to need no 3D experience
  • Subscriptions add custom watermarks, templates and more users

Pricing

From the pricing page, read 12 October 2026 (USD, excluding VAT):

  • Free account: up to 5 projects, limited to 3 floors and 3 designs total, with watermarked SD-quality exports
  • Credits for upgrading projects (higher-resolution exports, 3D tours): 5 for $8.15, 10 for $16.30, 30 for $48.90, 100 for $163.00
  • Basic, Plus and Pro subscriptions give monthly credits at a discount; their prices load dynamically and I could not read them, so they are not listed here

Pros

  • Free tier with no time limit
  • Pay-per-project credits avoid a mandatory subscription
  • Large model library included for everyone

Cons

  • Credit-based upgrades make the total cost depend on how many projects you finish
  • Free exports are SD and watermarked, and free-plan exports have a 10-minute cooldown
  • We have not tested drawing speed or render quality

Alternatives

See Homestyler, Coohom and RoomSketcher.
