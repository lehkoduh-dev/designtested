Foyr Neo Review 2026: Pricing, Features and Alternatives

Published 2026-07-27 · Facts checked 2026-10-13 · By the DesignTested team

Quick take

Best for: Interior designers and small studios that want floor planning, 3D modelling and rendering in a single application, and that value a free trial and clear render allowances.

Not ideal if: Occasional users or hobbyists on a tight budget, and heavy renderers who would need the Standard or Premium plan to get past 30 render credits a month.

Our verdict: Foyr Neo's published pitch is an all-in-one planning and rendering tool for designers, priced monthly by render credits. From the listed plans, it fits working studios better than casual users, and the cost depends on how many renders you need.

Based on the vendor’s published information; hands-on notes will be added.

What it is

Foyr Neo is a design application for interior designers that combines 2D planning, 3D modelling and rendering in one product. The vendor says it needs no plugins and offers HD, 2K and 4K renders on every plan, with 12K rendering on the top plan.

Who it’s for

Interior designers and small studios who want to produce rendered visuals for clients without assembling several tools.

Key features

  • Floor planning, 3D editing and modelling, and rendering in one app, per the vendor.
  • Render output up to 4K on Basic and Standard; 12K on Premium.
  • Team seats: the FAQ says Standard and Premium allow 2 and 5 members respectively.

Pricing

Published monthly prices as of 12 October 2026 (USD, excluding VAT/tax), per Foyr’s pricing page:

Plan Price Render credits
Basic $39/month 30 per month
Standard $89/month 180 per month
Premium $159/month Unlimited

A 14-day free trial is offered, and the vendor states no credit card is required. Foyr’s page also shows annual billing at a lower monthly rate; confirm the current figures before buying. Plans can be upgraded or downgraded at any time.

Pros

  • Rendering and planning in one product.
  • Free trial without a card.
  • Clear credit allowances per plan.

Cons

  • Higher monthly prices than most tools in this batch.
  • Basic plan has only 30 render credits per month.
  • Credit-based rendering means heavy users need Standard or Premium.

FAQ

Do I need a credit card for the trial? Per Foyr’s pricing page FAQ, no card details are required to start the 14-day free trial, and all Neo features can be used during it.

When am I billed and in what currency? The FAQ says your card is charged on the date of purchase and on each monthly or yearly anniversary. It lists USD, INR, CAD, GBP, AUD and EUR as accepted currencies, read 13 October 2026.

How does changing plan work? You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. An upgrade is pro-rated for the rest of the month, and a downgrade is credited on the next month’s bill, per the vendor.

Alternatives

  • Planner 5D for a cheaper plan with 4K renders on its Professional tier.
  • RoomSketcher for floor plans at a lower price.
  • SketchUp for general 3D modelling.
