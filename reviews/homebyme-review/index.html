HomeByMe Review 2026: Pricing, Features and Alternatives

Published 2026-08-13 · Facts checked 2026-10-13 · By the DesignTested team

Quick take

Best for: Homeowners and small design practices who want a browser-based 2D/3D planner with branded furniture and 4K images.

Not ideal if: Users who need construction-grade CAD drawings or fully offline software.

Our verdict: A free-to-start planner with clear tiers where heavier rendering and white-label output sit on paid plans; judged only on the vendor's published information.

Based on the vendor’s published information; hands-on notes will be added.

What it is

HomeByMe is described on its site as an online 3D space planning service developed by Dassault Systèmes SE. It lets users draw a 2D floor plan, decorate in 3D and produce images, with a free Starter plan to begin.

Who it’s for

HomeByMe’s FAQ says it is for individuals and professionals, naming real estate agents, architects, interior designers and developers. The site also has separate pages for interior designers, enterprise, brands and DIYers.

Key features

  • 2D floor plan creation or import, with 3D walkthrough using an avatar.
  • A catalogue of branded furniture and materials; the site cites 90k+ products on the pricing page and 300+ brands.
  • MakeByMe, a tool to build custom furniture.
  • Renderings in Full HD and 4K, plus 360° images and video on higher plans, with daylight, night mode and artificial lighting.
  • 3D model import, 2D plan export and Draftsight export on selected plans.
  • Sharing, a community Inspiration feed, and a mobile app.
  • The vendor states that products shown are sold by partners, not by Dassault Systèmes.

Pricing

Published on HomeByMe’s offers page, read 13 October 2026 (USD, tax treatment not stated):

Plan Monthly billing Annual billing
Starter Free Free
Essentials $29 per month $25 per month ($299 paid at once)
Pro $65 per month $53 per month ($640 paid at once)

Starter includes 2 projects and 5 Full HD images. Essentials adds unlimited projects, 50 Full HD, 5 4K and 5 Topview images per month. Pro adds unlimited Full HD, 4K and Topview images, 10 360° images, 60 seconds of Full HD video and 20 seconds of 4K video per month, plus logo removal and read-only projects. Enterprise pricing is on request.

Pros

  • Free tier to start; no installation needed.
  • Large branded catalogue and furniture-building tool.
  • Clear plan comparison, with credit-based rendering limits.

Cons

  • Watermark-free output and project protection are Pro features.
  • Render and video quantities are capped on lower plans, and credits are non-cumulative.
  • Some site pages still mention older plan names (Premium, Unlimited+), so check the current plan details before subscribing.

Alternatives

  • Coohom for a similar catalogue-driven planner.
  • Homestyler for online room design.
  • Foyr Neo for a design-focused rendering workflow.

FAQ

I am a professional, can I use HomeByMe? Yes. HomeByMe says it serves individuals and professionals such as real estate agents, architects, interior designers and developers, and points professionals to its plans for full capabilities.

What are renewable credits? Monthly subscribers are credited each month; yearly subscribers receive all yearly credits at once, for example 600 Full HD images on Essentials Yearly. Credits are non-cumulative.

Can I modify my subscription? Yes, from account settings. You can switch between monthly and yearly at any time and are charged pro rata for the previous plan.
