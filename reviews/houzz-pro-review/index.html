Houzz Pro Review 2026: Business Software for Designers and Contractors

Published 2026-08-17 · Facts checked 2026-10-13 · By the DesignTested team

Quick take

Best for: Contractors, remodelers and interior design firms that want proposals, invoicing, scheduling and client management in one system, with the design tools (3D floor plans, mood boards, selections) alongside.

Not ideal if: Solo designers who only need visualisation or rendering, or anyone who wants a low-cost, no-commitment tool, since paid plans start at $99 per month and the trial leads into an annual subscription.

Our verdict: Houzz Pro's published plans position it as a business-operations suite rather than a design tool, with design features in the entry plan and project, budget and marketing tools in higher tiers. Judged from its pricing page alone, it suits firms that will use the estimating and invoicing side, not those buying it for 3D drawing.

Based on the vendor’s published information; hands-on notes will be added.

What it is

Houzz Pro is business management software for contractors and design professionals. Its homepage describes it as built to run a firm under one roof, and says it is trusted by 3 million+ contractors and design pros.

Who it’s for

The site lists audiences including new construction, remodeling, interior design, architecture and painting, with tailored tools for builders, interior designers, remodelers and property owners.

Key features

The vendor’s feature menu groups tools as follows:

  • Planning: 3D floor plans, takeoffs, contracts, bid management, product clipper and library, selections, CRM, mood boards
  • Project management: schedule, files and photos, task management, AI note-taking, daily logs, procurement, time tracking, client and subcontractor dashboards
  • Financial: estimates, proposals, job costing, change orders, invoices, online payments, reports, QuickBooks integration, homeowner financing
  • Marketing: custom website, email marketing, premium profile, targeted advertising, lead generation
  • A mobile app

Pricing

Read on the vendor’s pricing page (www.houzz.com/houzz-pro/pricing) on 13 October 2026 (USD per month; the page does not say whether tax is included). The older pro.houzz.com/pricing address still returned a 503 error, so the figures come from this page:

  • Design: $99/month, 1 user. Lists 3D floor plans, selections, product clipper and library, mood boards, CRM, estimates and proposals, invoices and online payments
  • Pro: $199/month, 1 user. Everything in Design plus takeoffs, bid management, change orders, budget management, contracts, project schedule, daily logs and a Gusto integration (Beta)
  • Teams: from $399/month, unlimited users. Everything in Pro plus AI note-taking, premium profile, email marketing, custom website, front desk and verified caller (both Beta) and dedicated support
  • Additional users on Design and Pro: $50 per user per month, per the comparison table
  • Advertising package (add-on): from $499/month
  • A Free Plan is also advertised, described as exploring tools such as the 3D floor planner, estimates and invoicing; no credit card is required to try Houzz Pro for free

The page notes that additional charges may apply for selected add-ons. 3D model conversions are limited by a fair-use cap of 415 per month on higher plans, and Design and Pro list 5 monthly conversions.

Pros

  • Combines design, proposals, invoicing and project management in one system
  • Free plan, 30-day free trial and demo are offered
  • Built for firms that sell and manage projects, not only draw them

Cons

  • Entry plan starts at $99 per month for one user, with extra users at $50 per month
  • Less focused on rendering than dedicated visualisation tools
  • We have not tested it

FAQ

How does the free trial work? The vendor’s FAQ says you can use Houzz Pro free for 30 days and cancel at any time during the trial, before the annual subscription begins at the end of it.

Can clients pay through it? Yes. The FAQ says clients can pay through Houzz Pro by credit card, debit card or ACH bank transfer, and the payments are tracked inside the software.

Does it work with QuickBooks and mobile? The FAQ says it integrates with QuickBooks Online to sync estimates and invoices and avoid duplicate entries, and that a Houzz Pro mobile app is available for iOS and Android.
