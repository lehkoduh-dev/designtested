Live Home 3D Review 2026: Multi-Platform Home and Interior Design App

Last updated 2026-10-13 · By the DesignTested team

Quick take

Best for: Homeowners, DIY renovators and professionals who want floor plans, furnishing and 3D visualisation on Mac, Windows, iPad, iPhone or Android, and prefer a one-time licence to a subscription.

Not ideal if: Users who want browser-only access or a single bundled price, since each platform is a separate app and Standard and Pro features are sold separately.

Our verdict: Live Home 3D publishes a free download, lifetime licences and Standard and Pro tiers across platforms. From the vendor's feature chart, Pro mainly adds higher-resolution output and advanced tools, so simple room projects may not need it.

Based on the vendor’s published information; hands-on notes will be added.

What it is

Live Home 3D is home and interior design software from BeLight Software. The vendor describes it as multi-platform software for creating detailed floor plans and 3D renderings, laying out and furnishing rooms or whole houses, planning exteriors and adjusting terrain.

Who it’s for

The site targets general users and also has Business and Education sections. Standard and Pro feature sets are sold separately.

Key features

  • Floor plans and 3D visualisation in one app
  • Interior furnishing and exterior and terrain planning
  • Apps for macOS, Windows, iOS/iPadOS, visionOS and Android (Version 5 is announced on the site)
  • Additional 3D content packs sold in the store

Pricing

From the vendor store, read 12 October 2026 (USD, excluding sales tax/VAT; prices may vary with exchange rates). The store shows promotional prices against list prices:

  • Mac: Standard lifetime licence $59.99 (list $79.99); Pro lifetime licence $129.99 (list $149.99)
  • Windows, iOS/iPadOS and Android: app is free to download with in-app purchases. Re-reading the store on 13 October 2026, the Windows and iPhone/iPad pages list Live Home 3D Pro as a lifetime licence at $129.99 (list $149.99). The Standard and subscription prices I recorded on 12 October ($5.99/month or $19.99/year for Standard; $39.99/year for Pro) were not shown on those store pages on re-reading, so treat them as unconfirmed
  • The vendor says purchases made in-app on one platform become available on the others when you log in to the same free account, and offers a 30-day money-back guarantee
  • Free version: per the vendor, limited to two stories and one saved project, and exports carry a watermark

Pros

  • One-time lifetime licence is available, not only subscriptions
  • Runs on desktop, tablet and phone platforms
  • Free app download lets you look before paying

Cons

  • Features are split across Standard and Pro tiers, ; the vendor lists differences such as unlimited stories and higher export resolution in the feature chart
  • Promotional prices may change
  • We have not tested it

FAQ

Is there a free version? Yes. Per the vendor’s FAQ, the free version lets you try almost all Standard layout, building and furniture tools, but saves only one project and adds a watermark to exported images and walkthroughs.

Do I need to buy separately for each platform? Each platform is a separate app bought from its own store, but the FAQ says purchases made in one app become available on other platforms when you log in to the same free account.

What does the library include? The vendor’s FAQ cites over 2,400 customisable 3D objects and more than 2,100 surface materials, read 13 October 2026.
