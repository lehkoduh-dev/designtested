Live Home 3D Review 2026: Multi-Platform Home and Interior Design App

What it is

Live Home 3D is home and interior design software from BeLight Software. The vendor describes it as multi-platform software for creating detailed floor plans and 3D renderings, laying out and furnishing rooms or whole houses, planning exteriors and adjusting terrain.

Who it’s for

The site targets general users and also has Business and Education sections. Standard and Pro feature sets are sold separately.

Key features

  • Floor plans and 3D visualisation in one app
  • Interior furnishing and exterior and terrain planning
  • Apps for macOS, Windows, iOS/iPadOS, visionOS and Android (Version 5 is announced on the site)
  • Additional 3D content packs sold in the store

Pricing

From the vendor store, read 12 October 2026 (USD, excluding sales tax/VAT; prices may vary with exchange rates). The store shows promotional prices against list prices:

  • Mac: Standard lifetime licence $59.99 (list $79.99); Pro lifetime licence $129.99 (list $149.99)
  • Windows, iOS/iPadOS and Android: app is free to download with in-app purchases. Standard subscription $5.99/month or $19.99/year; Pro subscription is also offered monthly and yearly (yearly $39.99, list $49.99). Lifetime licences $59.99 (Standard) and $129.99 (Pro)

Pros

  • One-time lifetime licence is available, not only subscriptions
  • Runs on desktop, tablet and phone platforms
  • Free app download lets you look before paying

Cons

  • Features are split across Standard and Pro tiers, and I did not verify which features sit in which tier
  • Promotional prices may change
  • We have not tested it

Alternatives

See Sweet Home 3D, SketchUp and Chief Architect.
