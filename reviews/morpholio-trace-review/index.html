Morpholio Trace Review 2026: Sketching and Markup App for Designers

Last updated 2026-10-12 · By the DesignTested team

Based on the vendor’s published information; hands-on notes will be added. The vendor’s own website could not be reached when this page was prepared, so the details below come from the app’s official App Store listing.

What it is

Trace is a drawing and design app for iPhone and iPad. The listing describes it as an all-in-one sketching tool for developing ideas in layers and marking up drawings, and shows Vectorworks, Inc. as the seller.

Who it’s for

The listing names architects, interior designers, landscape architects, industrial designers, urban planners, lighting designers and engineers.

Key features

  • Draw on top of images, PDFs, maps, photos, drawing sets and 3D models, to scale.
  • Layers, a layer manager, scale pen, scale ruler and instant scale.
  • Export images, PDFs or sets; sketch recording.
  • Higher tiers add stencils, PDF markup and manager, perspective tools, maps to scale, smart fill, AR perspective finder, AR SketchWalk, sketch over 3D models and PSD export.

Pricing

Listed on the App Store page as of 12 October 2026 (USD, US store, sales tax not included). The app has a free tier (up to 5 projects, basic pens and brushes). In-app subscriptions are listed as:

Item Listed price
Trace Essentials Subscription $4.99
Trace Plus Subscription $9.99
Trace Pro Monthly $7.99
Trace Pro Subscription $24.99

The listing does not state the billing period for each item. One subscription covers iPhone and iPad. Payments are handled by Apple.

Pros

  • Free tier with layers, scale tools and export.
  • Tiered subscriptions; Plus allows up to 20 projects and Pro unlimited projects.
  • Works with PDFs and drawing sets for markup.

Cons

  • The listing describes iPhone and iPad; no other platform is mentioned.
  • Several useful tools (perspective, AR, 3D model sketching) are Pro-only.
  • Billing periods and the tier-to-price mapping are not clear from the listing.

Alternatives

SketchUp is a 3D modelling alternative; RoomSketcher focuses on floor plans.
