SketchUp Review 2026: Pricing, Features and Alternatives

Last updated 2026-10-13 · By the DesignTested team

Quick take

Best for: Designers, architects and educators who want a flexible 3D modelling tool with extensions, from a low-cost iPad and web plan up to Windows-only advanced workflows.

Not ideal if: Beginners who want a ready-made room planner or one-click renders; SketchUp is a general modelling tool with a learning curve.

Our verdict: SketchUp offers a broad tier ladder and a large extension ecosystem, which suits users who want to model custom geometry. It is not interior-design-specific, so judge whether its modelling depth is worth the learning curve.

Based on the vendor’s published information; hands-on notes will be added.

What it is

SketchUp is a 3D modelling application sold in tiers. The vendor lists Go, Pro and Studio as its main subscriptions, with further Pro variants for specific workflows.

Who it’s for

Designers and architects who want to build custom 3D models and are willing to learn a general modelling tool.

Key features

  • Go, Pro and Studio subscription tiers, each building on the previous one (“Everything in Pro, plus…” for Studio).
  • Pro variants for scanning, civil contracting and advanced workflows.
  • Education and enterprise licensing are listed separately.

Pricing

Published on SketchUp’s plans page read 13 October 2026 (USD, per user, billed annually):

Plan Price per month
Go $10.75
Pro $33.25
Studio $71.58
Pro Scan / Pro Civil Contractor $41.58
Pro Advanced Workflows $49.92

Monthly billing is also offered: the page listed Go at $19.99 and Pro at $99.99 per user per month, and showed no monthly price for Studio. Education pricing is $55 per user per year for students and for educators, and SketchUp for Schools (K-12) is listed as free with a Google Workspace for Education or Microsoft Education account. Check the page for the feature list of each tier and for taxes.

Pros

  • Wide range of tiers, from low-cost Go to Studio.
  • Flexible modelling not tied to a single room-planning workflow.

Cons

  • Annual billing is much cheaper than monthly (Pro $33.25 versus $99.99 per month).
  • A general modelling tool is not interior-design-specific; expect a learning curve.

FAQ

What is the subscription term? The vendor says it offers annual and monthly plans, paid at the start of the term and renewing automatically at the then-current price (plans page FAQ, read 13 October 2026).

What is the refund policy? For annual subscriptions, refund requests must be made within 14 days of the original transaction or renewal date; monthly subscriptions can be cancelled with a refund within 7 days of the first purchase.

How do I access a subscription? Through a Trimble ID; each account is tied to one person.

Alternatives

  • Foyr Neo for design-focused rendering.
  • Planner 5D for simpler room design.
  • Sweet Home 3D for a free option.
