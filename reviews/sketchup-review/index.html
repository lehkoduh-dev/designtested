SketchUp Review 2026: Pricing, Features and Alternatives

Based on the vendor’s published information; hands-on notes will be added.

What it is

SketchUp is a 3D modelling application sold in tiers. The vendor lists Go, Pro and Studio as its main subscriptions, with further Pro variants for specific workflows.

Who it’s for

Designers and architects who want to build custom 3D models and are willing to learn a general modelling tool.

Key features

  • Go, Pro and Studio subscription tiers, each building on the previous one (“Everything in Pro, plus…” for Studio).
  • Pro variants for scanning, civil contracting and advanced workflows.
  • Education and enterprise licensing are listed separately.

Pricing

Published on SketchUp’s plans page as of 12 October 2026 (USD, per user, billed annually):

Plan Price per month
Go $10.75
Pro $33.25
Studio $71.58
Pro Scan / Pro Civil Contractor $41.58
Pro Advanced Workflows $49.92

Education pricing starts at $55 per user per year. Check the page for the feature list of each tier and for taxes.

Pros

  • Wide range of tiers, from low-cost Go to Studio.
  • Flexible modelling not tied to a single room-planning workflow.

Cons

  • Prices are quoted for annual billing only.
  • A general modelling tool is not interior-design-specific; expect a learning curve.

Alternatives

  • Foyr Neo for design-focused rendering.
  • Planner 5D for simpler room design.
  • Sweet Home 3D for a free option.
