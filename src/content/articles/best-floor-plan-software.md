Floor plan tools turn a room into a drawing you can show a client. This list covers seven that we reviewed, from free to professional.

## How we chose

We looked at what each vendor publishes: whether there is a free plan, the entry price, and whether the tool produces 2D plans, 3D views and renders. Ranking reflects price and coverage on paper, not hands-on testing.
