Interior designers use laser distance measurers for site surveys: room lengths and widths, ceiling heights, window and door openings, and the quick area and volume figures that feed a floor plan, a flooring order or a paint estimate. A laser does this faster than a tape and lets one person measure a whole room. Models with Bluetooth can send readings straight to an app.

## How we chose

We picked five current models from well-known tool makers, each listed on the manufacturer's own website as of 2026-10-12. All specs below are based on manufacturer-published specifications. We have not hands-on tested any of these devices. Prices are shown only where the manufacturer's page publishes one; none of these pages did, so the price column is left empty rather than filled from retailers.

## 1. Bosch GLM 50-27 CG

**Our pick.** On published specs it is the only model here that combines a green laser, Bluetooth with an app, a 0° to 360° incline sensor and IP65 protection, while keeping the same 1.5 mm typical accuracy as the 100 m models. For room-sized interior work, 50 m of range is plenty. This is a judgement from spec sheets, not from testing, and no price is published to weigh it against.

Bosch lists a range of 0.05 to 50 m, typical accuracy of ±1.5 mm, a 515 nm green laser and Bluetooth 4.2 Low Energy for use with the Bosch MeasureOn app. It stores 30 values and Bosch says it withstands 1.5 m drops.

## 2. Hilti PD-I

Hilti describes the PD-I as a robust laser meter for interior applications up to 100 m, with a measuring range of 0.05 to 100 m and 1.5 mm accuracy. It offers single and continuous measurement.

Its Bluetooth connection is documented for partner apps including magicplan, imagemeter, Floor Plan Creator and WinWorker, which is useful if you already sketch plans on a phone or tablet.

## 3. DeWalt DW03101

DeWalt lists the DW03101 with a 100 m working range and ±1.5 mm accuracy per 10 m. It calculates distance, area, volume and indirect height (Pythagoras), and has a colour display described as visible in all working conditions.

It connects over Bluetooth to the DEWALT Tool Connect app for more features, is rated IP54 for water and debris resistance, and has a 1/4 in thread for tripod mounting.

## 4. Milwaukee 2282-20

Milwaukee lists the 260 ft Laser Distance Meter with a range of 2 in to 260 ft and a basic accuracy of 1/16 in. It offers seven measurement functions: length, area, volume, indirect measurement (Pythagoras), stake-out, timer and horizontal mode.

It keeps the last 20 measurements in memory and ships with a soft carrying case. We found no Bluetooth listed on the page, so it suits designers who note readings by hand. It uses imperial units on the US page.

## 5. Bosch GLM 40

Bosch lists the GLM 40 with a range of 0.15 to 40 m and typical accuracy of ±2.0 mm. It covers standard length, surface and volume calculation as well as indirect height measurement, and shows the last three results on an illuminated display.

It uses a red 635 nm laser, saves 10 measurements, weighs about 0.1 kg and is rated IP54. It has no Bluetooth, so it is the simplest option here. This page is on Bosch's Australian site.
