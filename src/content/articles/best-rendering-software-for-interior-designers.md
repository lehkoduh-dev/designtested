Rendering turns a model into the image a client says yes to. The right tool depends on where your model lives: in CAD/BIM software, or in an all-in-one design app.

## How we chose

We compared published prices, free options and the workflow each tool describes. We have not benchmarked render quality or speed.

| Tool | Price | Free option |
|---|---|---|
| [D5 Render](/reviews/d5-render-review/) | Pro plans from $38/month | Yes |
| [Enscape](/reviews/enscape-review/) | Solo $47.90/month, billed annually | No |
| [Lumion](/reviews/lumion-review/) | Pro named user $1,149/year | No |
| [Foyr Neo](/reviews/foyr-neo-review/) | From $39/month (Basic) | Trial |
| [Blender](/reviews/blender-review/) | Free | Yes |

## 1. D5 Render — Our pick for low cost

Real-time rendering with a free Community plan (non-commercial) and Pro plans from $38/month. The lowest cost of entry on this list.

![D5 Render](/img/tools/d5-render-review.jpg)

<small>Image: Screenshot of the official website, 12 October 2026</small>

[Read the full review →](/reviews/d5-render-review/)

## 2. Enscape — Our pick for CAD/BIM workflows

Works inside your CAD or BIM application, so model changes show in the rendered view as you make them. Solo is $47.90/month billed annually.

![Enscape](/img/tools/enscape-review.jpg)

<small>Image: Chaos (official website)</small>

[Read the full review →](/reviews/enscape-review/)

## 3. Lumion

Real-time rendering from a CAD or BIM model, with AI tools and a cloud space for review. Pro named user is $1,149/year.

![Lumion](/img/tools/lumion-review.jpg)

<small>Image: Lumion (official website)</small>

[Read the full review →](/reviews/lumion-review/)

## 4. Foyr Neo — Our pick for all-in-one

Combines 2D planning, 3D modelling and rendering in one app, aimed at interior designers. From $39/month with a 14-day free trial.

![Foyr Neo](/img/tools/foyr-neo-review.jpg)

<small>Image: Foyr (official website, product demo)</small>

[Read the full review →](/reviews/foyr-neo-review/)

## 5. Blender

Free and powerful, but a general 3D tool: expect to build and light scenes yourself.
