The Blender download page lists **Blender 5.2.2 LTS**, released on 15 September 2026, as the current version, with Blender 5.3 shown as upcoming. The 5.2 series is described by the Blender Foundation as having node-powered physics and online asset libraries, with two years of long-term-support updates.

Blender is free and open source. It is a general 3D tool rather than interior-specific software; see our [Blender review](/reviews/blender-review/) for who it suits.
