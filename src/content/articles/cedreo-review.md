## What it is
Cedreo is a home design tool for drawing floor plans and producing renderings. It offers a Free plan and Personal, Professional and Enterprise tiers.

## Who it's for
Home builders, remodelers and designers who need house plans and visuals for clients.

## Key features
- Free plan: one project with basic floor plan drawing tools.
- Personal and Pro plans include rendering allowances; extra renderings can be bought and do not expire at the end of the period, per the FAQ.
- Pro and Enterprise monthly rendering credits expire at the end of each period.

## Pricing
The text of [Cedreo's pricing page](https://cedreo.com/pricing/) we could read on 12 October 2026 did not show plan amounts, so we publish none. Per the vendor's FAQ: Free accounts are kept for 3 years and Personal accounts for 10 years unless you upgrade; plans cannot be downgraded after subscribing; and annual billing cannot be switched back to monthly.

## Pros
- Free plan to try the drawing tools.
- No credit card needed to sign up.

## Cons
- Free plan is limited to one project.
- Plan prices were not readable in the page text.
- No downgrades once subscribed.

## Alternatives
- [Foyr Neo](/reviews/foyr-neo-review/) for interior rendering.
- [RoomSketcher](/reviews/roomsketcher-review/) for floor plans.
- [Planner 5D](/reviews/planner-5d-review/) for home design with renders.
