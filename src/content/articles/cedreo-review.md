## What it is
Cedreo is an online home design tool for drawing floor plans and producing renderings. It offers a Free plan and Personal, Professional and Enterprise tiers, and the FAQ says nothing needs to be downloaded.

## Who it's for
Home builders, remodelers and designers who need house plans and visuals for clients.

## Key features
- Free plan: one project with basic floor plan drawing tools.
- Personal and Pro plans include rendering allowances; extra renderings can be bought and do not expire at the end of the period, per the FAQ.
- Pro and Enterprise monthly rendering credits expire at the end of each period.

## Pricing
From [Cedreo's pricing page](https://cedreo.com/pricing/), read in a rendered browser on 13 October 2026 (USD; tax treatment not stated):

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 1 user, 1 project, essential floor plan drawing |
| Personal | $59 per project, pay once | 20 renderings and 3 AI renderings per project |
| Pro | $59/month billed monthly, or $39/month billed annually | Unlimited projects, 40 renderings/month, 10 AI renders |
| Enterprise | $69/user/month billed monthly, or $59/user/month billed annually | Team tools, 80 renderings and 20 AI renderings per user/month |

Per the vendor's FAQ: Free accounts are kept for 3 years and Personal accounts for 10 years unless you upgrade; plans cannot be downgraded after subscribing; and annual billing cannot be switched back to monthly.

## Pros
- Free plan to try the drawing tools.
- No credit card needed to sign up.
- Prices, including a one-off per-project Personal option, are published on the pricing page.

## Cons
- Free plan is limited to one project.
- No downgrades once subscribed.

## FAQ

**What payment methods does Cedreo accept?**
Credit card (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and PayPal, per the vendor's FAQ (read 13 October 2026).

**What happens to unused renderings?**
On Pro and Enterprise, unused monthly renderings expire and do not roll over. Additional renderings you buy do not expire.

**Can I cancel and get a refund?**
You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period, but the FAQ says no refund is issued for the unused period.
