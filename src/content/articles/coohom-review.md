## What it is

Coohom is an online home design platform that combines a 2D floor planner, a 3D room designer and cloud rendering. Its site describes a workflow of drawing or uploading a floor plan, furnishing it in 3D, and producing rendered images, with AI-assisted layout and room design tools added on top.

## Who it's for

The vendor targets homeowners and design professionals, and also offers Business and Enterprise options and an affiliate/partner programme. Kitchen, closet, bathroom and office planners are listed as dedicated product pages.

## Key features

- 2D floor plan drawing, or starting from an uploaded plan or template
- Real-time 3D design with a library of 600,000+ 3D models, per the homepage
- Rendering described as "photorealistic" and available without installing software
- AI home and room design tools, plus an AI 3D generator
- Windows and Mac apps, a mobile app and an upload tool for brand models

## Pricing

Coohom's pricing page (read 12 October 2026) lists three plans: Basic (free), Pro and Elite. Read in a rendered browser on 13 October 2026 (USD; taxes not stated): Pro is $29/month billed monthly, or $25/month billed yearly ($300/year). Elite is $69/seat/month billed monthly, or $58/seat/month billed yearly ($699/year). Basic allows 3 projects and 1K watermarked renders; Pro and Elite remove the watermark. Enterprise is priced by contacting the vendor.

## Pros

- A free Basic plan exists
- Runs in the browser, with apps also available
- Floor planning, 3D design and rendering are in one product

## Cons

- Pricing pages run limited-time offers, so amounts may change
- We have not tested output quality, render times or model accuracy

## FAQ

**Can my team work together on Coohom?**
Per the vendor's FAQ (read 13 October 2026), the Elite plan suits small teams sharing projects and a model library.

**Do I need SketchUp skills?**
No. The FAQ says no software skills are needed, and that SketchUp .skp models can be uploaded to add your own assets.

**Can I cancel or change plans?**
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time, but there are no refunds for unused partial months.
