## What it is
D5 Render is a real-time rendering application from D5. The vendor presents it as part of a family that includes D5 Lite (a visualization plugin with AI mode) and D5 Works (an asset library).

## Who it's for
Architecture, interior and landscape designers who want rendering, animation and interactive presentations. The vendor lists SketchUp, Revit, Rhino, 3ds Max, Cinema 4D, Archicad, Blender and Vectorworks as supported tools.

## Key features
- Scene editing: PBR material editing, lighting controls, terrain and ocean, scatter and path tools.
- Image and panorama output up to 16K; video up to 4K on Community and Pro, 8K on Teams.
- Panorama, spatial and interactive presentation tours.
- AI features, including an AI agent, with limits that vary by plan.
- Official assets: 2,100+ on Community, 16,000+ on Pro and Teams.
- Teams adds multi-editor scene editing, comments, member access control and 100 GB cloud space.

## Pricing
As of 12 October 2026 the [D5 site](https://www.d5render.com/) states "Free for individuals" and "Pro plans from $38/month". The pricing page lists Community, Pro, Teams and Education plans; Education is free for students and educators. Per-plan prices on the pricing page were not readable in the page source, so check the live page for Pro and Teams amounts and for tax treatment.

The Community license is for non-commercial use (learning, personal projects, evaluation); commercial work requires Pro.

## Pros
- Free Community plan with unlimited scenes.
- Free education plan.
- Detailed plan comparison table.

## Cons
- Community is limited per the comparison table: 1 tour and 1 presentation, limited AI agent use, 2,100+ official assets and 10 GB cloud space.
- Commercial use needs a paid plan.
- Exact Pro and Teams prices could not be confirmed from the page source.
