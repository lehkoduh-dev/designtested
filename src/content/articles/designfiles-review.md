## What it is

DesignFiles is a web-based system for running an interior design business. The vendor describes it as connecting design work, sourcing, invoicing and client communication in one place.

## Who it's for

Practising interior designers and decorators. Its two paid plans are aimed at online (e-design) designers and at full-service designers and decorators; an Enterprise tier is for larger teams.

## Key features

- Moodboard editor, floor plans and 3D renderings.
- Product clipper to save items from vendor websites, plus a library the vendor says holds over 2M products.
- Client portal with branded presentations, approvals and feedback.
- Contracts, questionnaires, project calendar and task management.
- On the Full Service plan: quotes, invoices and retainers, purchase orders, request for quotes, spec sheets, product tracker, financial reporting and QuickBooks integration.

## Pricing

Published on designfiles.co/plans on 12 October 2026 (figures shown on the page; the page does not state VAT treatment):

- e-Design: 55 per month
- Full Service: 75 per month
- Additional users: +$30 per month each
- Enterprise: call for pricing

Both paid plans list unlimited projects. The site says there is no annual contract and offers a 90-day money-back guarantee. The homepage also offers a free trial.

## Pros

- Covers visuals and the business side (quotes, invoices, purchasing) in one system.
- Month-to-month billing with a stated 90-day money-back guarantee.
- Unlimited projects on both paid plans.

## Cons

- Per-user fees add up for teams.
- Invoicing, purchase orders and spec sheets are only on the more expensive Full Service plan.
- The vendor's testimonials and statistics are its own marketing claims, not independent evidence.
