## What it is
Enscape is a real-time visualization tool from Chaos. The vendor describes it as a design companion that works inside your CAD or BIM application, so changes to the model show up in the rendered view as you make them.

## Who it's for
Designers and architects who already model in one of the supported applications and want visuals without exporting to a separate program. The vendor's page lists Revit, SketchUp, Rhino, Archicad and Vectorworks.

## Key features
- Real-time rendering and walkthroughs inside the host application, with a bi-directional link to the model.
- Chaos Cosmos asset library: up to 6,700 render-ready assets on Solo, up to 10,000 on Premium, more than 18,500 on Collection.
- Veras AI for exploring design options, plus a monthly allowance of Chaos AI credits that grows by plan.
- Cloud collaboration through Chaos Cloud.
- Higher plans add Enscape Envision (animation and scene assembly) and Enscape Impact (sustainability analysis).

## Pros
- Works inside tools many designers already use.
- Plans are listed with prices and included assets and credits.
- Free trial available.

## Cons
- Needs one of the supported host applications; it is not a standalone modeller.
- Named personal license, billed annually at the listed prices.
- Cost rises with each tier, and the AI allowance depends on the plan.

## Alternatives
[Lumion](/reviews/lumion-review/) and [D5 Render](/reviews/d5-render-review/) are other real-time renderers. [SketchUp](/reviews/sketchup-review/) is a common host application.

## FAQ

**Who is Enscape best suited for?**
The vendor's FAQ (chaos.com/enscape, read 13 October 2026) says architects and interior designers who want real-time visualization in a single CAD environment.

**How does it fit my existing workflow?**
It plugs into your CAD or BIM application with bi-directional data flow, so model changes are reflected in Enscape immediately.

**How do teams share visualizations?**
Via Chaos Cloud, which the vendor says enables 3D scene sharing, live comments and annotations through a web link.
