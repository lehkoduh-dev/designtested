## What it is

Homestyler is a cloud-based 3D home design tool. Its homepage describes designing 3D homes online with over 1 million furniture models and cloud rendering, with no installation needed.

## Who it's for

The site has separate landing pages for interior designers, homeowners, schools, brands and retailers, and real estate. Team plans are aimed at design firms.

## Key features

- Cloud 3D floor planner
- Rendering: unlimited 1K on the free plan; paid plans add 2K/4K renders, panoramas and video
- Tools listed on the site include an interior design agent, image-to-image, image-to-moodboard and image/text-to-3D (AI features)
- Paid plans add watermark removal, uploads of your own textures and 3D models, multi-floor, custom lighting and coloured floor plan / construction drawing export
- A model and template asset market, and the option to sell design services

## Pricing

Published on Homestyler's pricing page, read 12 October 2026 (USD; tax treatment not stated):

- Basic: free
- Pro+: from $6.8/month
- Master+: from $11.8/month
- Team: from $19.6/seat/month

Pay-as-you-go renders are also listed, for example a 2K image at $0.99 per render and a 4K image at $1.99. The page shows "starts from" prices, so the price you pay depends on the billing option you choose.

## Pros

- Free plan with unlimited 1K renders
- Low entry price for paid tiers
- Rendering can be bought per item without a subscription

## Cons

- 4K renders on the free plan must be purchased each time
- Prices are shown as "starts from", which makes the real cost harder to compare
- We have not tested model quality or render results

## FAQ

**Is there a free plan?**
Yes. The vendor's FAQ describes a freemium model with a free Basic plan that includes unlimited projects, a library of free 3D models and unlimited 1K renders. Some rendering features can be unlocked with Homestyler Coins earned through activities such as daily login.

**How do I cancel?**
Per the FAQ, go to My Subscription in your Workspace and choose Cancel Auto Renew on the membership card. The subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period.

**Are there education plans?**
The vendor says it offers special plans for schools and universities, handled by contacting its business email address. Read 13 October 2026.
