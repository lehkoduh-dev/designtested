## What it is

Houzz Pro is business management software for contractors and design professionals. Its homepage describes it as built to run a firm under one roof, and says it is trusted by 3 million+ contractors and design pros.

## Who it's for

The site lists audiences including new construction, remodeling, interior design, architecture and painting, with tailored tools for builders, interior designers, remodelers and property owners.

## Key features

The vendor's feature menu groups tools as follows:

- Planning: 3D floor plans, takeoffs, contracts, bid management, product clipper and library, selections, CRM, mood boards
- Project management: schedule, files and photos, task management, AI note-taking, daily logs, procurement, time tracking, client and subcontractor dashboards
- Financial: estimates, proposals, job costing, change orders, invoices, online payments, reports, QuickBooks integration, homeowner financing
- Marketing: custom website, email marketing, premium profile, targeted advertising, lead generation
- A mobile app

## Pricing

Houzz Pro has a Pricing link, but the pricing page returned a server error (503) when I tried it on 12 October 2026, so I could not read any published prices and none are quoted here. The homepage offers a free trial, a free account and a demo.

## Pros

- Combines design, proposals, invoicing and project management in one system
- Free trial and demo are offered
- Built for firms that sell and manage projects, not only draw them

## Cons

- Price could not be verified
- Less focused on rendering than dedicated visualisation tools
- We have not tested it
