Floor plan software looks similar from the outside: draw walls, drop in furniture, export a picture. The differences show up in what you can deliver to a client, how the tool charges you, and how much time you spend learning it. This guide gives you a short way to narrow the field. For our shortlist, see [Best Floor Plan Software](/best/best-floor-plan-software/).

## Start with what you must deliver

Write down the files a client or contractor actually receives from you. Typical outputs are a 2D plan with dimensions, a furnished layout, a 3D view, and sometimes a CAD export. Each tool is built around some of these and not others.

- **Plans and simple visuals:** [RoomSketcher](/reviews/roomsketcher-review/) produces 2D and 3D floor plans and 3D photos, and is focused on plans and visuals rather than full rendering or detailed modelling.
- **Plans plus renders and exports:** [Planner 5D](/reviews/planner-5d-review/) lists unlimited watermark-free 4K renders, custom 3D model uploads and CAD export on its Professional plan.
- **Rendering-first design work:** [Foyr Neo](/reviews/foyr-neo-review/) combines 2D planning, 3D modelling and rendering in one product, per the vendor.
- **Custom modelling:** [SketchUp](/reviews/sketchup-review/) is a general 3D modelling tool, so expect a learning curve.

## Understand the pricing model

The same monthly price can mean very different things. Check which of these models a tool uses.

- **Flat subscription.** Planner 5D lists Premium at $19.99/month and Professional at $49.99/month (its FAQ figures; the pricing cards show different amounts, so confirm at checkout).
- **Credits.** RoomSketcher Pro includes 5 credits a month and Team 20, used to create projects, floor plans and 3D photos. [Foyr Neo](/reviews/foyr-neo-review/) gives 30 render credits a month on Basic ($39/month) and 180 on Standard ($89/month), with unlimited on Premium ($159/month).
- **Pay per project.** [Floorplanner](/reviews/floorplanner-review/) sells credits for upgrading projects, for example 5 for $8.15 or 30 for $48.90, so cost follows how many projects you finish.
- **One-time licence.** [Live Home 3D](/reviews/live-home-3d-review/) offers lifetime licences, for example $59.99 for Standard on Mac, alongside subscriptions on other platforms.
- **Free.** [Sweet Home 3D](/reviews/sweet-home-3d-review/) is free and open source.

All prices here are from the vendors' pages as read on 12 October 2026 and can change. Cedreo's plan amounts were not readable on its page, so we list none; check its [review](/reviews/cedreo-review/) for the plan structure.

## Match the tool to your platform

Check where you work. Sweet Home 3D runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android, and Live Home 3D lists macOS, Windows, iOS/iPadOS, visionOS and Android. [Coohom](/reviews/coohom-review/), [Homestyler](/reviews/homestyler-review/) and Floorplanner run in the browser with no installation. If you survey on site with a phone, [magicplan](/reviews/magicplan-review/) captures floor plans on a phone or tablet, though its marketing is aimed at restoration teams rather than designers.

## Test with a real room

Free plans and trials exist so you can try this. Draw one room you already know, with real dimensions, and note:

- How fast you can enter exact wall lengths, doors and windows.
- Whether the furniture library has the kind of pieces you specify.
- What the free export looks like. Floorplanner's free exports are SD and watermarked, for instance.
- Whether you can export what your client or contractor needs.

## Checklist before you buy

- [ ] I know which files I must hand to clients (2D plan, 3D view, render, CAD).
- [ ] I understand how the tool charges: flat, credits, per project or one-time.
- [ ] The plan I need allows commercial use and removes watermarks.
- [ ] It runs on the devices I actually use.
- [ ] I drew a real room in the free plan or trial.
- [ ] I checked the current price on the vendor's page, including tax.

## If you are on a tight budget

Start free and upgrade only when a client deliverable demands it. Our [free software roundup](/best/best-free-interior-design-software/) covers what is available at no cost, and Sweet Home 3D, RoomSketcher's free plan and Planner 5D's free plan are the obvious places to begin.

Whatever you pick, remember that the plan is only as good as the measurements you feed it. See our guide to measuring a room for a floor plan, and if rendering is part of your offer, read rendering for interior designers first.
