## What it is

Interior AI is a web app that takes a photo of a room and generates redesigned versions of it. Its homepage says you can redesign, furnish or reimagine interiors, exteriors and gardens, and get first results "in less than a minute."

## Who it's for

The site targets homeowners and professionals who want fast visual ideas, and it names real-estate virtual staging (furnishing empty homes) as a use case. For a designer, it is a concept-visualisation tool rather than a drawing or documentation tool.

## Key features

- Upload a photo, or a sketch or SketchUp screenshot, and choose a room type and a style.
- Modes listed on the site include interior design, style transfer, virtual staging, sketch-to-image, SketchUp screenshot, outdoor design and "empty the space."
- A custom-style option lets you upload a reference photo of a style you like.
- Higher plans list editing tools (paint walls, change lighting, add furniture or people, upscale) and walkthrough video generation.

## Pricing

Published on interiorai.com on 12 October 2026, in USD (the site does not state VAT treatment):

| Plan | Monthly billing | Yearly billing (per month) | Designs per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | $49 | $29 ($349/year) | 1,000 |
| Premium | $99 | $49 ($599/year) | 5,000 |
| Ultra | $199 | $99 ($1,199/year) | 25,000 |

Pro includes a commercial use licence. Render quality, parallel renders and extra tools rise with each plan. We did not find a free plan on the page.

## Pros

- Quick way to produce style variations from an existing photo.
- Commercial use licence listed from the lowest plan.
- Virtual staging and sketch inputs are covered.

## Cons

- Subscription only, with no free tier visible on the pricing section.
- Editing tools, video and higher render quality sit on the Premium and Ultra plans.
- The vendor flags its prompt feature as experimental and says it may give random or bad results.
