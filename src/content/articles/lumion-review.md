*Based on the vendor's published information; hands-on notes will be added.*

## What it is
Lumion is real-time 3D rendering software. The vendor describes a workflow from CAD or BIM model to visuals, with AI tools and a cloud space (Lumion Cloud) for sharing and review.

## Who it's for
Architects, interior designers, landscape architects and urban planners who want to turn a model into images, videos and panoramas. Lumion lists interior design among its use cases.

## Key features
- Free Lumion LiveSync plugin to keep a CAD/BIM model in sync with the scene; the site lists SketchUp and Revit among its integrations.
- 10,000+ models and materials, plus terrain, nature, lighting, weather and effects.
- Outputs: images, videos and 360 panoramas for VR review.
- AI tools: image enhancer, material generator and image upscaler, included in every plan.
- Lumion Cloud: upload and share renders, collect comments, compare versions.
- Axogram, a free live-diagramming plugin, is listed as a separate product.

## Pricing
Published on [Lumion's buy page](https://lumion.com/product/buy) as of 12 October 2026 (USD, billed upfront for one year; the page does not state whether taxes are included):

| Plan | Per month | Billed upfront (1 year) |
|---|---|---|
| Lumion Pro, named user | $96 | $1,149 |
| Lumion Pro, floating seat | $125 | $1,499 |

A 3-year plan is listed with a 20% saving. Custom licensing is offered for 10 or more users. A free Pro trial is available. The buy page, re-read 13 October 2026, also lists a 14-day full-refund guarantee, free onboarding, and a floating-seat plan that includes 24/7 premium support. Axogram, the live-diagramming plugin, is marked as a new free plan.

## Pros
- AI tools and cloud collaboration are included in the listed plans.
- Free trial and a free LiveSync plugin.
- Floating-seat option for teams.

## Cons
- Paid plans are billed upfront for the term.
- Only Lumion Pro is listed as a paid product on the buy page; no lower-cost tier is shown.

## FAQ

**Is there a free trial or refund?**
Yes. The buy page lists a free Lumion Pro trial and a full refund within 14 days, with no questions asked, read 13 October 2026.

**What is the difference between the two plans?**
Per the vendor, the floating seat includes all Lumion Pro features plus use on multiple computers, sharing across a team and 24/7 premium support. The named-user plan is for individual professionals.

**What about larger teams?**
Custom licensing is offered for practices of 10 users or more, with volume licensing, premium support and onboarding, handled through the vendor's sales team.
