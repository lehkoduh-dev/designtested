## What it is
Planner 5D is a 2D/3D home design editor that runs on web and mobile. The vendor also lists AI plan recognition and an AI Studio with separate credit allowances.

## Who it's for
Homeowners and enthusiasts (Premium) and interior designers or studios doing client work (Professional), per the vendor.

## Key features
- Free plan: unlimited projects, floor plans, 2D/3D design and 50% of the furniture catalogue.
- Premium: full catalogue of 10,000+ premium items.
- Professional: unlimited watermark-free 4K renders, custom 3D model uploads, CAD export, a Specs & Docs Organizer and a Price Estimator.
- Project sharing and shared editing on every plan.

## Pricing
Published on [Planner 5D's pricing page](https://planner5d.com/pricing) read 13 October 2026 (USD, excluding taxes):

- **Free:** $0.
- **Premium:** $19.99/month, or $59.99 billed annually.
- **Professional:** $49.99/month, or $399.99 billed annually.

The pricing cards on the page show different per-month figures than the FAQ; the amounts above come from the FAQ, so confirm at checkout.

## Pros
- Free plan with no time limit.
- Professional adds renders, CAD export and cost estimating.

## Cons
- Free plan has half the catalogue.
- Inconsistent price displays on the pricing page.

## FAQ
**Can I use it for free?** Yes. The vendor's pricing FAQ (read 13 October 2026) says the Free plan costs $0 with no time limit and covers floor plans, 2D and 3D design, sharing and 50% of the furniture catalogue.

**How do AI credits work?** AI plan recognition and AI Studio use separate allowances. Paid Premium includes 100 AI Studio credits with monthly billing or 1,200 with annual billing; Professional includes 1,000 monthly or 12,000 annual, per the vendor.

**Is it free for schools?** Verified teachers and professors can apply for a free Education program, with verification and limits applying, according to the vendor.
