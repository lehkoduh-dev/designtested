## What it is
RoomSketcher is an online floor plan and home design tool. It produces 2D and 3D floor plans and 3D photos, and also offers an AI floor plan conversion feature (one conversion is included in the free plan, per the vendor).

## Who it's for
Designers, home stylists and real estate users who mainly need floor plans and simple visuals.

## Key features
- 2D and 3D floor plans and 3D photos.
- A credit system: Pro includes 5 credits per month and Team 20, replenished on the 1st of each month. Credits are used to create projects, floor plans, 3D Photos and other outputs; more can be bought.
- Pay-per-item options are also listed on the pricing page (for example $4 per render).

## Pricing
Published on [RoomSketcher's pricing page](https://www.roomsketcher.com/pricing/) as of 12 October 2026 (USD, excluding VAT/tax):

- **Free:** $0, with one free AI floor plan conversion.
- **Pro:** $24/month billed monthly, or $12/month billed as $144 per year.
- **Team:** $70/month billed monthly, or $35/month billed as $420 per year.

## Pros
- Free plan to start with.
- Low entry price for Pro.
- Annual billing is half the monthly rate.

## Cons
- Output volume is capped by monthly credits.
- Focused on plans and visuals rather than full rendering or detailed modelling.
