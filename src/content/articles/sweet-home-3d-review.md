*Based on the vendor's published information; hands-on notes will be added.*

## What it is
Sweet Home 3D is a free, open-source interior design application for drawing a plan of your home, placing furniture and viewing the result in 3D.

## Who it's for
Individuals and beginners who want to sketch layouts at no cost.

## Key features
- Draw rooms and place furniture on a plan, with a 3D view.
- Available on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android, and online in a WebGL-capable browser, per the vendor.

## Pricing
Free and open source, according to the [official website](https://www.sweethome3d.com), read 13 October 2026. The vendor FAQ says it is free for personal or commercial use under the GNU General Public License, with optional donations, and that paid store versions exist on the Microsoft Store, Mac App Store, Google Play and Apple App Store. We did not verify store prices.

## Pros
- No cost.
- Runs on many platforms.
- Open source.

## Cons
- Not aimed at polished client-ready rendering compared with paid tools.
- Less suited to team and proposal workflows.

## FAQ
**Is it really free?** According to the vendor FAQ (read 13 October 2026), you can use the installer or online version free of charge for personal or commercial purposes under the GNU General Public License.

**What does the store version add?** The vendor says it includes over 10,000 3D models and 400 textures and, compared with the online version, can import plug-ins.

**What are the system requirements?** The FAQ lists Windows 98 to Windows 11, Mac OS X 10.4 to macOS 14, Linux and Solaris, with a 400 MHz processor, 256 MB of memory and a graphics card with an updated driver as the minimum.

## Alternatives
- [RoomSketcher](/reviews/roomsketcher-review/) for a free tier with a hosted workflow.
- [Planner 5D](/reviews/planner-5d-review/) for a bigger catalogue and renders.
- [SketchUp](/reviews/sketchup-review/) for detailed modelling.
